# CryptoSignal AI v0.6.2

- Live market cards refresh every 15 seconds while the app is open.
- Rolling 24h percentage now prefers live exchange data (Binance/Kraken/Coinbase where available) and falls back to CoinGecko.
- Initial load fetches fast market data before the heavier intelligence dashboard.
- Added a prominent mobile-friendly ACTIONABLE NOW summary with current BUY/SELL signals and confidence.
- Scan Now remains a full market + news + order-book scan and now runs GDELT discovery/order-book work concurrently to reduce waiting.
- Added explicit market update timestamps and stale/reconnecting status behavior.


## v0.6.3 — Railway database crash fix
- Normalizes Railway/Postgres `postgres://` URLs to SQLAlchemy-compatible `postgresql://`.
- Tolerates accidental surrounding quotes in `DATABASE_URL`.
- Prevents an unresolved Railway variable reference from crashing startup; falls back to the bundled SQLite database and logs a warning.
- Adds `pool_pre_ping` for more resilient PostgreSQL connections.
- Health endpoint version updated to 0.6.3.
